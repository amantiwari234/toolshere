# ❤️ For My Favorite Human — Romantic Love Website

A cute, modern, romantic, and interactive website created specially for my girlfriend to express how much I love her.

## ✨ Features
- 💖 **Interactive Love Story Scrapbook**: Real photo memories with handwritten washi tape polaroids.
- 🎶 **Background Music & Spotify Anthem**: Plays *"I Think They Call This Love"* (Cover by Matthew Ifield) with spinning vinyl record and official Spotify widget.
- ⏳ **Live Relationship Time Counter**: Counts up days, hours, minutes, and seconds in love.
- 📸 **Multi-Frame Photo Showcase**: Heart-shaped frame, glowing neon halo, and polaroid styles with quick photo switching.
- 🏃‍♂️ **The Runaway "No" Button**: Dodges cursor/taps with hilarious excuses, making the "YES" button grow bigger!
- 🏆 **World's Best Girlfriend Certificate**: Custom modal with confetti shower and download/print support.
- 🤫 **Secret Thoughts Flip Wall**: 3D interactive flip cards with heartfelt confessions.
- 💍 **Interactive Ring Proposal Card**: Velvet gift box that opens with a gleaming ring and lifetime promise.
- 📜 **Wax-Sealed Love Envelope**: Unfolds into an intimate love letter.
- 🎟️ **Redeemable Love Coupons**: Cute vouchers for cuddles, late-night snacks, and back massages.
- 🧪 **Love Compatibility Meter**: Calculates compatibility score up to 10,000%.
- 🫙 **Jar of Reasons**: Interactive jar drawing sweet reasons to love her.
- 🕯️ **Candlelight Night Mode**: Toggles into a cozy midnight blackberry ambiance.

## 🚀 Live Demo & Hosting
Host instantly with **GitHub Pages**:
1. Go to repository **Settings** > **Pages**
2. Under **Build and deployment** > **Branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`
3. Click **Save** — Your website will be live at `https://rachit282.github.io/lovegenerator/`!
