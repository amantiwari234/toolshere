About QR Code Generator & Scanner & Core Utility

ToolsHere QR Code Studio & Scanner is an all-in-one, 100% client-side utility designed for creators, businesses, event coordinators, and everyday users. Create high-contrast, error-corrected QR codes for website URLs, Wi-Fi networks (with password escaping and visibility toggle), vCard business contacts, email drafts, and direct WhatsApp chats. Switch to the Scanner tab to decode QR codes directly from image uploads, rasterized SVGs, or your device camera in real time. Designed with a strict client-side security architecture, your camera stream, Wi-Fi passwords, and scanned results remain strictly in your browser memory with zero server tracking.

How to Use QR Code Generator & Scanner (Step-by-Step Guide)

  1. 1 Choose 'Generator' to create a QR code or 'Scanner' to read an existing code.
  2. 2 In Generator mode, select your content type (URL, Text, Wi-Fi, vCard, Email, or WhatsApp) and fill in the details.
  3. 3 Customize module and background colors, check contrast, and download in high-res PNG, vector SVG, or printable PDF.
  4. 4 In Scanner mode, drop an image file (PNG, JPG, SVG) or click 'Start Camera Scanner' to point your camera at a physical QR code.
  5. 5 Review decoded content safely with 1-click Copy, or preview verified HTTP/HTTPS links with zero automated redirects.

Frequently Asked Questions (QR Code Generator & Scanner)

How do I create a Wi-Fi QR code that guests can scan to connect?

Select the 'Wi-Fi' tab, type your network SSID name, choose your security type (WPA/WPA2/WPA3 or WEP), and enter your password. Guests scanning with their phone camera will see an instant 'Join Network' prompt without typing the password.

Can I scan a QR code from a picture or screenshot without a camera?

Yes! Switch to the 'Scanner' tab and upload your image (PNG, JPG, WebP, or SVG). Our client-side decoder analyzes the pixel data directly in your browser and reveals the encoded text instantly.

Does the scanner automatically open links found in QR codes?

No, never. For your online security, all decoded content is displayed as text first. If a valid web link is detected, you are shown an explicit 'Visit Link' button so you can inspect the destination before opening it.

Do the QR codes generated on ToolsHere ever expire?

No! These are standard static QR codes. Because the information is directly encoded into the visual matrix without redirects, they work forever.

Is my camera video stream or uploaded photo sent to a server?

No, 100%. Camera video frames and uploaded images are processed entirely inside your local device's memory using JavaScript and Web APIs. Nothing is ever recorded or uploaded.

Can I print these QR codes on banners, packaging, and business cards?

Yes. You can download vector SVG files for infinite print scaling with crisp edges, or high-resolution PNGs up to 2400px (4K print quality).
