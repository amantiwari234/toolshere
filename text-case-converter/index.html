Frequently Asked Questions (Text Case Converter)

What does the 'url-slug' transformation do?

It converts your headline into lowercase, removes punctuation marks, and replaces spaces with hyphens, creating clean, SEO-friendly URL permalinks.

Can I use Title Case for blog post titles and headlines?

Yes. Title Case capitalizes the first letter of each word, ensuring uniform styling for headings, essays, and titles.

No. You can convert short phrases or multi-page documents without restriction.
