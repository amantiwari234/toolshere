Architecture & Data Flow

How In-Browser Processing Works

Unlike legacy cloud converters that upload your confidential files to remote servers, ToolsHere executes supported document and image operations locally inside your web browser engine.

01 Local Input

Select Your File

Standard browser FileReader and File.arrayBuffer() APIs read selected file data into browser memory for local processing. No file upload payload is created or dispatched to a remote conversion farm.

Stays in local device memory
02 Client Engine

Process in Your Browser

Document parsing, image resizing, color selection, or text extraction runs locally using PDF.js, HTML5 Canvas, and Web Crypto APIs. Processing runs directly on your device rather than through a server-side conversion queue.

Rendered via client-side JavaScript
03 Instant Output

Download Your Result

The converted document or optimized image is packaged as a local Blob URL and saved straight to your computer or phone. Your source files never linger on an external hard drive or cloud bucket.

Direct browser blob download
Transparency Notice: In-browser processing applies to supported document and image conversion utilities. Standard web traffic (static assets, anonymous usage metrics, and Google AdSense advertising) operates over the network in compliance with our Privacy Policy.

Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding our client-side file compression, PDF conversion, background removal, and calculator utilities.

Image Compressor & Resizer

Image Compressor & Resizer: How does the target size preset (20KB, 50KB, 100KB) work?

The tool automatically calibrates the image compression ratio and resolution algorithms in browser memory to aim for your selected target threshold, making it easy to comply with government form restrictions.

Image Compressor & Resizer: Are my photos uploaded to any external server?

No. The entire compression and resizing pipeline runs inside your browser via the HTML5 Canvas API. Your pictures never leave your local device.

Image Compressor & Resizer: What output format will my compressed image be saved in?

The compressor exports as a standard JPEG (.jpg) file, which provides the best balance of file size reduction and image clarity for online portals.

Image Compressor & Resizer: Can I resize the width and height without losing quality?

Yes. You can enter specific pixel values into the width and height input fields. Downscaling dimensions also dramatically reduces the final file weight.

Background Remover

Background Remover: What types of photos work best with this background remover?

Photos with solid, plain, or uniform studio backgrounds (such as white, grey, green screen, or studio product shots) yield the crispest results.

Background Remover: Does downloading the image add any watermarks?

No. All images downloaded from ToolsHere are 100% free of watermarks, branding, or restrictions.

Background Remover: Why did parts of my subject disappear?

If the subject shares very similar colors with the background, lowering the Sensitivity Threshold slider will protect those pixels from being removed.

PDF to Word Converter

PDF to Word Converter: Can I open the downloaded .doc file in Microsoft Word and Google Docs?

The downloaded .doc file contains HTML-based Word-compatible content. It can be opened in Microsoft Word and LibreOffice Writer, although Word may display a file-format warning. Compatibility with other document editors may vary.

PDF to Word Converter: Is there a limit on how many PDFs I can convert?

No. You can convert as many PDF files as you need throughout the day without fees or daily caps.

PDF to Word Converter: Are my confidential files stored anywhere?

Never. The conversion happens in your local browser runtime. Your files are not transmitted to or stored on any server.

Merge & Split PDF

Merge & Split PDF: How many PDF documents can I combine at once?

You can select multiple files at once. Memory availability on your device is the only practical constraint.

Merge & Split PDF: Does this merger alter the visual layout or fonts of my PDFs?

No, original page streams, formatting, vector text, and embedded graphics are maintained intact.

Merge & Split PDF: Do I have to register an account or subscribe?

No registration, credit card, or personal information is ever requested.

Age & Date Calculator

Age & Date Calculator: Does this calculator account for leap years?

Yes. The algorithm dynamically checks leap years (such as February having 29 days) and exact month lengths for 100% calendar precision.

Age & Date Calculator: Why is the total days lived count useful?

Official government recruitments, medical evaluations, and insurance policies frequently specify age eligibility criteria strictly in total elapsed days.

Age & Date Calculator: Can I calculate past historical dates other than birth dates?

Yes. You can select any past date to compute the exact elapsed time between that event and today.
