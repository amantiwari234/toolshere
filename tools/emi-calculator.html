How Loan EMI is Calculated

Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is the fixed monthly amount a borrower pays to a lender on a specific date each month. It consists of both principal and accrued interest components.

Mathematical EMI Formula:
EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ] / [(1 + r)ⁿ - 1]

Where P = Loan Principal Amount, r = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100), and n = Total Number of Monthly Installments.

During the initial months of your loan, a larger proportion of the EMI goes toward interest payment. Over time, as the principal diminishes, the interest component decreases and the principal contribution increases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Three main factors determine your EMI: the total loan amount (principal), the interest rate charged by the bank or financial institution, and the repayment tenure in years or months.

A longer tenure reduces your monthly EMI amount, making monthly cash flows more manageable. However, it significantly increases the overall interest paid over the life of the loan.
