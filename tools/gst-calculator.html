Standard GST Slabs in India

The Indian GST Council divides goods and services into five primary tax brackets:

GST Slab Intra-State Split Typical Items & Services
0% (Nil) CGST 0% + SGST 0% Fresh vegetables, milk, eggs, unbranded grain, basic healthcare, education
3% / 0.25% CGST 1.5% + SGST 1.5% Gold, silver, precious jewelry (3%); rough precious stones (0.25%)
5% CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5% Packaged food items, tea, coffee, edible oil, life-saving drugs, economy air travel
12% CGST 6% + SGST 6% Processed food, butter, cheese, cell phones, computers, business class air tickets
18% CGST 9% + SGST 9% Software services, IT consulting, telecom, restaurant dining, financial services, hair oil
28% CGST 14% + SGST 14% Automobiles, luxury cars, cement, tobacco, high-end consumer electronics

Frequently Asked Questions

For sales occurring within the same Indian state (Intra-State), tax is divided equally into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). For sales between different states (Inter-State), Integrated GST (IGST) is levied in full and shared between Centre and State.
