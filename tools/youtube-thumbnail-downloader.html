Standard YouTube Thumbnail Dimensions

According to YouTube Creator recommendations, custom video thumbnails should satisfy the following specifications:

  • Recommended Resolution: 1280 × 720 (with a minimum width of 640 pixels).
  • Aspect Ratio: 16:9 widescreen format, which matches the video player on both desktop and mobile.
  • Supported File Formats: JPG, GIF, or PNG.
  • File Size Limit: Under 2MB for standard videos; under 10MB for podcasts.

Note: In rare cases where a video was uploaded in low resolution or without a custom thumbnail, YouTube only creates the 480p HQ and 320p MQ fallback images.
