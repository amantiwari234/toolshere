SEO Meta Tags Best Practices

Search Engine Optimization starts with clean, semantically sound metadata in the HTML <head> container:

  • Page Title: Keep between 50 and 60 characters so Google does not truncate your brand and keywords in SERP results.
  • Meta Description: Keep between 150 and 160 characters. Provide an actionable call to action (CTA) and clear value proposition.
  • Canonical URL: Prevents duplicate content penalties by indicating the master source URL to search crawlers.
  • Open Graph & Twitter Cards: Increases click-through rates (CTR) on social media platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, X, and WhatsApp.
