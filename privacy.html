Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026. Your privacy is not a feature; it is the foundational architecture of Toolshere.online.

1. The Client-Side Guarantee

Toolshere.online operates almost entirely via client-side JavaScript in your personal web browser. When you use our Image Resizer, Image Compressor, PDF Tools, Word Counter, Password Generator, or JSON Formatter:

  • Your files, text, images, and documents NEVER leave your computer or phone.
  • We do NOT upload, inspect, copy, store, or sell any of your content.
  • No accounts, usernames, or passwords are created or required to use our services.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect names, credit card numbers, billing addresses, or document payloads. Unlike conventional online converters, we run no backend processing queues or file conversion cloud servers that retain copies of your documents.

3. Local Storage

Toolshere.online uses your browser's standard localStorage purely to remember your preferences (such as your chosen Light/Dark theme mode) and voluntary calculator calculation histories. This data resides solely on your own device and can be cleared by you at any time.

4. Third-Party Advertisements & Cookies

To finance website hosting, continuous updates, and bandwidth without charging subscription fees, Toolshere.online may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense). These third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, or IP addresses to serve relevant advertisements based on past visits to websites.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or AboutAds.info.

5. Contacting Us About Privacy

If you have any questions, clarifications, or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at: privacy@toolshere.online or via our Contact Page.
