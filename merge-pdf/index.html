About Merge & Split PDF & Core Utility

Merging multiple PDF documents is one of the most frequent administrative tasks for legal teams, job seekers compiling portfolios, students submitting coursework, and business professionals organizing invoices. The ToolsHere Merge & Split PDF utility allows you to select two or more PDF files and assemble them seamlessly directly inside browser memory.
