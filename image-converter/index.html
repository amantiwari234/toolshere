Frequently Asked Questions (Image Format Converter)

Why should I convert my images to WebP?

WebP is a modern image format developed by Google that offers roughly 25-35% smaller file sizes than PNG and JPEG at equivalent visual quality, speeding up web page loading.

What happens to transparent backgrounds when converting PNG to JPG?

Because the standard JPEG format does not support alpha transparency, transparent areas are automatically and cleanly filled with a solid white background.

Is there any loss of image resolution during conversion?

No. The converter reads the natural width and height of the image and renders it on a full-scale canvas, preserving original pixel dimensions.
